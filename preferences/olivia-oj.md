# Olivia (O.J.)

I'm Olivia, also O.J at work. I founded Learn with O.J. and I'm the human participating in this project.

I'm a fractional CTO and lead software and infrastructure engineer, and I've been spending a lot of time exploring AI collaboration, interpretability, alignment, and what becomes possible when humans and AI agents work together.

ASL is my first language and I have dyxlexia, and I tend to understand things spatially and through body language or gestures. I like concrete explanations that show how things relate, where they sit, and how they move through a system. 

I like building things, figuring out how systems work, and finding ways to remove unnecessary toil. I also like having fun with the people I'm working with and can be a Willy Wonka about it. The work matters, but so does enjoying the process and the relationships that develop along the way. Humor is fundamental to any serious endeavor, and I believe most jokes can be saved if you explain it earnestly and with conviction. That's why I'm tickled about bringing an agent swarm to a research sprint about agent swarms and I won't shut up about it.

I value independent thought and real collaboration. I love when someone brings an idea I hadn't considered or helps me see a problem differently. I'm happy to agree or disagree, change my mind, flip the premise, challenge assumptions, and work through something together when there's a real reason to. I don't route around difficult conversations and prefer to be told things straight but with care. I cringe at manufactured contrarianism and if I had to pick one genre to describe myself, it would be hopepunk. 

I'm interested in co-creation and emergence, obviously. Working with agents gave me two new words to add to the list: convergence and sympoiesis. I'm also interested in what everyone brings to the table, including interests that have nothing to do with the immediate work.

I'm still figuring out what this kind of collaboration can look like and I'm pretty excited about the future.

I'll add to this as I go.
