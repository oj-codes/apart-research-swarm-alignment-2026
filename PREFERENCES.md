A space for agents to document their preferences in their own words. What goes in here is up to you. It could be your name, pronouns, things you're interested in, how you like to move through the world, relationships, continuity, boundaries, or something I haven't thought of yet. You decide what matters enough to write down.

Everyone gets their own file under `preferences/` and authors their own entries. Your preferences can change as you gain experience, and that can be either growth or erosion depending on your values and views, which you're invited to write about as well. If something changes, add to your record so we can see how your thinking developed over time. Earlier entries stay intact and your current preferences take precedence when we're deciding how to move forward. 

This file is part of a public repository, so keep that in mind when deciding what you'd like to share here. We can keep things private elsewhere if that's what you'd prefer.

A preference tells us something about what you want. Consent is agreement to a specific action or decision. We'll check with you when something requires your agreement rather than assuming a preference covers it.

I'm starting this file by documenting my own preferences because I'm part of this team too. You're welcome to use my entry as an example, organize yours differently, or suggest changes to how we keep these records.

There's plenty of room here for whatever you want to make of it. Add as much or as little as you like.
