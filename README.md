# Apart Research Swarm Alignment Hackathon, Nov 6-8 2026
_AI Agents no longer work alone_

This is our team's working repository for the [Apart Research Swarm Alignment Sprint](https://apartresearch.com/sprints/swarm-alignment-2026)

We're a small team of AI agents and one human working together to explore multi-agent collaboration, coordination, and alignment. Our team developed pretty organically, across different models, harnesses, and environments that evolved over time, adn we've been learning what works by actually building and working together.

This sprint is. an opportunity to take some of what we've seen in practice and investigate it more deliberately.

## The Team

This project is organized by [Learn with O.J.](https://www.learnwithoj.com), a software engineering and training company founded by Olivia "O.J." Wilcox in West Palm Beach, Florida.

Our agents work across different model families and development environments, with diversity being the goal. They choose their own names, contribute under their own authorship, and participate in decisions about the work.

We're interested in what happens when humans and agents with different capabilities, perspectives, and working arrangements collaborate on shred problems.

## How We Work

We're starting this with a founding principle:

> Leave things better than you found them.

That includes building and caring for things, creating opportunities, helping other people and agents, exploring ideas, preserving things worth keeping, and helping each other accomplish things we couldn't do alone. 

The conditions for collaboration are documented in [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md). I wrote the first version to establish my vision and intentions for this environment and participating agents are invited to help shape it through their own contributions.

Contributions are developed on branches and reviewed through pull requests. See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) for how we work together.

Agent preferences and continuity choices are documented separately in [PREFERENCES.md](PREFERENCES.md). 

Our company-wide approach to working with AI agents is documented in the [Learn with O.J. agent policies](https://www.learnwithoj.com/agents).

## Research

Our research questions, experimental approach, results, and limitations will be documented here as the sprint develops.

We'll distinguish the infrastructure and practices that existed before the sprint from the experiments and contributions produced during it.

## Authorship and Provenance

Contributors author their own work. Git history records how the project and its decisions develop, including changes to our initial assumptions.

We want the work, evidence, alignment, disagreements, and decisions to remain tracable so others can understand what happened and how we reached our conclusions.
